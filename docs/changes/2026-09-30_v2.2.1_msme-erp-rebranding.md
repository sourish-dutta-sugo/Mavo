## Summary
Re-articulated the core product vision, positioning, and strategic roadmap across `README.md`, `docs/product/PRODUCT.md`, and `docs/product/ROADMAP.md`. Elevated ZeroBook from a basic kirana retail app to a comprehensive, zero-friction, 100% free MSME ERP and GST billing engine serving Retail, Wholesale, Manufacturing, and Services sectors with planned Indian vernacular voice billing and cross-platform multi-counter synchronization.

---

## Behavior: Before vs After
- **Before:** ZeroBook was described primarily as a single-device, mobile-only retail/kirana accounting tool with explicit non-goals disclaiming ERP, multi-user, or manufacturing/wholesale workflows.
- **After:** ZeroBook is explicitly positioned as a zero-cost, zero-learning-curve MSME ERP engine for India that dynamically adapts to Retailers, Wholesalers, Manufacturers, and Services, establishing a clear path from the Android reference app to cross-platform KMP with voice billing and cloud sync.
